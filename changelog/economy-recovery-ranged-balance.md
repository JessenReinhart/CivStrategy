# Economy recovery and ranged combat balance

- A village with food and enough housing now recovers 1 happiness per game second at 0% tax. This lets population growth recover after a crisis. Starvation, crowding, and high-tax penalties still apply; 20% tax remains neutral. The tax panel explains the recovery conditions.
- Archers, slingers, and chariots now benefit from the military damage research they were already promised.
- Ranged volleys retain fractional damage against armor. Splitting an attack into visual arrows no longer multiplies minimum damage or flat armor research, so armor cannot make a volley hit harder.
- Ranged attacks use the same defensive modifiers in normal play and stress simulations.
